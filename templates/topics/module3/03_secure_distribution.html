Secure Distribution (Public Keys)

To solve the Key Distribution Problem, we must stop using the same key to lock and unlock our data. Welcome to Asymmetric Cryptography, where every user generates a mathematically linked pair of keys.

The Padlock Metaphor

In symmetric encryption (AES), sending a key over the internet is like mailing a physical house key. Anyone who steals the envelope can copy the key. Asymmetric encryption completely changes the rules:

🔓 The Public Key

Think of this as an open padlock. You can safely send this padlock to anyone in the world over an unsecured internet connection. Even if Eve intercepts it, she just has an open padlock. It is completely safe to share.

🔑 The Private Key

Think of this as the physical metal key. It never leaves your computer. Once someone uses your Public Key padlock to lock a box, your Private Key is the only thing in the universe that can open it.
