Module 2

Introduction to Symmetric Encryption

Symmetric encryption is the workhorse of modern cryptography. It is incredibly fast and efficient, relying on a beautifully simple concept: a single, shared secret key used to both lock and unlock the data.

What You Will Learn

1. The Shared Key Concept

Explore the foundation of symmetric systems. Just like a physical padlock, the exact same key that secures the data is required to open it.

2. The AES Standard

Get introduced to the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES). Learn why governments, banks, and the military trust this specific algorithm to protect their most sensitive files.

3. The Key Distribution Problem

Discover the fatal flaw of symmetric encryption. If Alice and Bob are across the world, how can they safely share the secret key over the internet without a hacker stealing it?
