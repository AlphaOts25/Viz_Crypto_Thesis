Module 1

Introduction to Encryption & Decryption

Encryption and decryption are the basic processes that make secure communication possible. Encryption changes readable information, called plaintext, into an unreadable form called ciphertext. This prevents unauthorized people from understanding the data even if they are able to access or intercept it. Decryption reverses the process by converting the ciphertext back into its original readable form, but only when the correct key or method is used.

In this module, learners will understand how messages are protected as they move from one person or system to another. The focus is not only on memorizing definitions, but also on visualizing how information is transformed, protected, transmitted, and recovered. By learning encryption and decryption first, students build the foundation needed to understand more advanced cryptographic concepts such as ciphers, secret keys, key exchange, and secure communication.

What You Will Learn

1. The Encryption Process

Learn how readable information is transformed into a hidden, unreadable format to protect it from unauthorized access.

2. The Decryption Process

Learn how encrypted information is converted back into its original readable form using the correct key or method..

3. Caesar Cipher

Learn a simple classical cipher that shifts letters in the alphabet to create encrypted messages.
