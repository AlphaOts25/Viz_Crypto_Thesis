Module 1

Introduction to Encryption & Decryption

Welcome to the foundation of cryptography. In this module, we explore the core mechanics of how data is scrambled to hide its meaning from unauthorized eyes, and how it is unscrambled back into readable format.

What You Will Learn

1. Plaintext vs Ciphertext

Learn the vocabulary of cryptography. Understand the difference between the original readable message and the scrambled, unreadable output.

2. The Encryption Process

Visualize how an algorithm and a secret key transform $m$ (message) into $c$ (ciphertext). We will look at the mathematical formula $E(k, m) = c$.

3. The Decryption Process

See how the receiver reverses the process. We will explore the formula $D(k, c) = m$ and why the key is the most critical piece of the puzzle.
